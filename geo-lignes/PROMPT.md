# Prompt (méthode du guide « Every Prompt From the Video »)

```
Create a 15 second vertical product video for a set of 4 black metal geometric wall shelves from etageres-murales.fr, using HyperFrames.

PRODUCT
- A set of 4 wall shelves made of a thin black steel wire frame (black powder coat) with black MDF boards.
- Promise: one thin line is enough to dress a flat wall.
- Show these points in this order, all from the Shopify listing:
  Sizes: large 43 x 32 cm, long 40.5 x 15 cm, 2 small 15 x 20 cm, all 12 cm deep.
  Material: steel wire, black powder coat, black MDF boards.
  Included: 8 screws and 8 wall plugs. Price: 59,90 € for the set of 4.
- Never state a weight limit (the listing gives two different values).

STORY
- Hook, sizes, material, in a room, price. You design the scenes and write the words.
- One idea runs through the whole video: a single thin black line, like the steel wire. It enters on the
  empty wall, draws the four frames, and the drawn frames become the real shelves in the photos.
- Every scene transforms into the next one: the camera dives through the large drawn frame into the
  close-up photo, pulls back into the wide shot, pans on into the bedroom, and the last photo shrinks
  into the price card. Nothing fades or cuts.
- End on the price, the logo and "Lien en bio".

FORMAT
- 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps, exactly 15 seconds, MP4. Top 250 px and bottom 400 px free of text.

LOOK
- Calm, precise, premium. Background #F5F0E8, text #2C2420, one accent only, #C8A27C. Inter.
- Real product photos from the Shopify listing, full frame. No photo showing a weight claim.
- Avoid: gradients, stock icons, more than one accent color, anything that looks like a template.

MOTION
- The camera is always moving. Something new every half second.
- The line starts fast and lands soft. Labels pop in with a small overshoot.

SOUND
- Sound effects from the project library only, no music, no voice.
- Soft whoosh while the line travels, a click when each frame closes, a pop for each word,
  a deep whoosh on the dive, a low hit on the price, a chime on the logo.
- Every sound sits on the exact frame of the thing it belongs to.

QUALITY CHECK
- Render it and look at a frame every half second.
- Critique it like a senior motion designer: story, layout, spacing, type, timing, readability,
  every number against the listing, and whether the sound matches the picture.
- Fix everything, render again, critique again. At least three full rounds.

RULES
- Don't stop and don't ask me anything. Give me the final MP4, confirm it has sound, and list what
  you fixed in each round.
```
